**200 ppi** (the hard ceiling — no better scan exists). Transcribed/translated by a **5-read Gemini

## Prosopography recovered (with support)
- **Testator: Ambrogio Fagnani** (*Ambrosius de Fagnano dictus Faruffinus*), *filius q. Iohannis*;
  father **Giovanni**, mother **Iacobina** (c.3, single read). Builder/enlarger of S. Giacomo.
- **Wife: Giovannina / Iohannina** (c.1, 3/5; c.13 contested with a "Francische" misread → daughter's
  name, likely a confabulation swap).
- **Children** (as Ambrogio's heirs/legatees): priest son **Giovanni** (*presb. Iohannes*, → later
  **PREVOSTO di S. Giacomo**, c.14); daughters **Caterina, Margarita, Isabella, Lucrezia, Beatrice,
  Francesca, Lucia** (a **presbitera/nun Margarita**). Also named as heirs/substitutes across the
  charters: **Giovanni Giacomo (Iohannes Iacobus)** and **Giovanni Battista (Iohannes Baptista)** —
  whether distinct sons or cross-read variants of the same names is UNRESOLVED at 200 ppi.
- **Notary of the original will: Iohannes de [la Porta?], notarius Mediolanensis** (cited c.12 + c.14,
  two independent reads; surname [?]). **Dowry-instrument notary: Iohannes Pietro de Carcano** (c.13,
  1/5). **Copyist of this exemplum: Donato Bonarico** (c.14, 4/5 + pixel).
- **Painter: Giovanni Agostino da Lodi** (*Iohannes Augustinus de Laude*), c.12 — matches published
  art-history (Prima Saronno; Biscottini & Crippa 1989) for the S. Giacomo altarpiece.
- Neighbours/toponyms: **Ludovico & Giovanni de Castiglione** (Castiglioni) *ad clusum* (c.2, s4-only);
  the **Bozzente** torrent (c.9); property in Gerenzano, Cislago, Turate, Uboldo, Saronno, Mozzate,
  Legnano, Busto Arsizio.

## The core, well-supported content (safe to cite)
Ambrogio Fagnani's **1512** testament(s), buried at **S. Giacomo di Gerenzano** in a sepulchre he
built, endowing the **completion of the church**: the wooden **ancona** for the high altar (Madonna
enthroned with Child, Ss. Peter, Paul, James, John the Baptist, a music-making angel — by **Giovanni
Agostino da Lodi**), the **presbytery vault frescoes** (God the Father centre, four Evangelists, four
Doctors), and the **bell tower with two bells** — all corroborated by published sources on S. Giacomo.
Heir line runs through the priest son **Giovanni**, who became provost of the very church his father
built. Everything beyond this core is flagged per page.
