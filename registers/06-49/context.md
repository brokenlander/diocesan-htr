Prepositurale dei SS. Apostoli Pietro e Paolo in Gerenzano 1896"* (Feste/Legati di S. Antonio, S.
Giacomo, S. Carlo, etc., with dates back to 1543 and £-sums). The bulk is **19th-century
administrative Italian** — legato records, receipts, accounts, correspondence — interspersed with
**older Latin quotations / earlier-dated documents** copied or bound in (cited dates range
1543–1896). Mixed EXIF orientations (many orientation-8, a couple genuinely landscape single
leaves — folded account slips, NOT open-book spreads). A few small folded slips are photographed on
a light/patterned background with fingers holding the page.

Reader guidance: identify each page's period/language from the script. 19th-c. Italian → light
modernization on the deliverable; Latin quotations → fluent modern Italian with key terms in
parentheses. Money often in **lire italiane (£)** and centesimi (also older lire/soldi/denari in
copied documents). Keep names/sums exactly at the pixels; `[?]` for one uncertain word, `[…]` for an
illegible run; flag words cut at the photo edge rather than guess.

Recurring families here: **Fagnani** (Marchese Fagnani; the campestre S. Giacomo is their oratory),

# Glossary — Archivio Parrocchiale di Gerenzano (fondi VI–XII)

Parish archive of **Gerenzano** (church of **SS. Pietro e Paolo**, prepositural/collegiate),
**Pieve d'Appiano**, diocese of Milan, on the Como/Varese border. Material spans **c. 1430–1896**.
Use to resolve hard proper names/places/formulae **only when the pixels support it — the image
always wins.** `[?]` = one uncertain word, `[…]` = an illegible run; never invent.

## Place
Gerenzano; Pieve d'Appiano. Nearby: Cislago, Turate, Uboldo, Saronno (Serono), Mozzate, Carbonate,
Rescaldina, Bregnano, Olgiate Olona, Legnano, Fagnano. Church chapels/altars: maggiore (SS. Pietro
e Paolo), S. Caterina, S. Zeno(ne), **S. Stefano** (iuspatronato Crivelli), the campestre **S. Giacomo**
(Fagnani oratory), S. Vittore, S. Ambrogio (ruined), Cappella del Cimitero (19th c.).

## Families (recurring; keep the scribe's spelling)
**Crivelli / de Cribellis**; **Gibelli / de Gibellis** (DISTINCT family — do NOT merge with Crivelli);
**Fagnani** (later the **Marchese Fagnani**; aliases **Faruffini, Ramponzani, di Louchi**); **Caimo /
Caymo**; **Carcano**; **Visconti** (= Vicecomes); **Castiglioni**; **Corno** (= de Comitibus / de'
Comiti); **Moneta**; **de Ferrari**; **Serafini**; **Bossi**; **Carnelli**; **Galimberti**; **Bonomi**;
**Rimoldi**; **Torri**; also Sevesi, Cattaneo, Ajroldi, Berra, Vanelli, Morandi, Pagnano, Galli.

## People across eras
- **Girolamo (Hieronimo) Armiraglio / Amiraglio**, provost from 1566. **Card. Carlo Borromeo**
  (visited 1574). **Giuseppe Trizio** = provost 1662.
- 19th c.: Prevosto **Luigi Garavaglia**; the **Marchese Fagnani**; agrimensore **Pietro Tagliabue**;
  **Pietro Gaggia** (Milan procuratore); **Carlo Rimoldi**, **Torri**; fabbriceri.

## Document types & formulae
*legati* (bequests / annuali di messe), *decime* (tithes), *contenzioso*, *benefici*, *inventari*,
*culto*, relic authentications. Land: **pertiche**; grain: **moggia, staia, mistura** (segale+miglio).
Boundaries: *coheret a mane / a meridie / a sera / a monte* = E / S / W / N. *q[uon]d[am]* = the late
(fu). Money: lire/soldi/denari imperiali (L./s./d.), scudi, **£ italiane / centesimi** (19th c.).
