# Register X 5 (1642–74) — context glossary  ·  slug: `x-5-1642-74`

> Injected into every Gemini read for X5. Grows as we learn. The image always wins over this glossary.

## What this register is
**1642–74 episcopal/synodal & pastoral-visitation decrees** of the Pieve d'Appiano, incl. **Gerenzano
(SS. Pietro e Paolo)**. Mostly **clean documentary Latin prose** — a later, generally more legible hand
than X4/X44. A few pages were transcribed earlier (p346/p348/p350/p353, Claude-direct).

## Carries over (same plebe/parish — see `registers/x-4-1574` & `x-44-1583` context for the full glossary)
- Gerenzano = collegiate **SS. Pietro e Paolo** (prepositura + 5 canonicates). Recurring families:
  Fagnani, Crivelli, Caimo, Carcano, Visconti, Castiglioni, Gibelli. Places: Cislago, Uboldo, Saronno,
  Mozzate, Turate, Olgiate Olona, Como, Rovello, Bregnano, Lurate/Marinone.
- ⚠️ By 1642–74 the clergy/provost are a **later generation** than the 1574–83 Armiraglio era — don't
  assume the same names; read each from the page.

## Doc type & formulae
Visitation/synodal acts & decrees: *"Visitavit ecclesiam … decernit / mandat / praecipit ut …"*; status
descriptions; inventories; index/tabula pages (folio refs). Heavy Latin abbreviation (eccl.a, p[res]b[yte]r,
S[anctissi]mo, q[uon]d[am], R[everendissi]mus, Ill[ustrissi]mus).

## Pitfalls
- Long-s (ſ) reads as f; near-blank/divider pages → transcribe only real text (bleed-through is not text).
- Index/tabula pages: keep the folio numbers; treat faint left-margin numerals as later archival marks.
- Figures/dates faint or cut → `[?]`, never invent.
