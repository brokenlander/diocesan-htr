# Register X 44 (1583) — context glossary  ·  slug: `x-44-1583`

> Injected into every Gemini read for X44. Grows as we learn. The image always wins over this glossary.

## What this register is
The **1583 pastoral visitation** (+ added documents) of the **collegiate & parish church of SS. Pietro
e Paolo, Gerenzano**, Pieve d'Appiano, diocese of Milan. Mostly **documentary Latin** (visitation acts),
some Italian. ~2 MP phone photos; faded; near-blank pages often struck with a diagonal line.
- Visitor: **Bernardino Taurino** (= Taurinus / Tauruzio / Taurutius), J.U.D., **delegated visitor of
  Card. Carlo Borromeo** (Archbp Milan, title S. Prassede). Visitation opened **13 July 1583**
  (cf. his sequestration mandate of 2 Aug 1583 in X4 p204 — same man).

## Carries over from X4 (same parish/area) — see `registers/x-4-1574/context.md` for the full glossary
- **Families:** Fagnani (aliases Faruffini / Ramponzani / di Louchi), Crivelli (Marcantonio, Ludovico,
  Bapta Pompeo de Cribellis), Caimo, Carcano, Corno, de Ferrari, Vigani, Gibelli, Visconti, Castiglioni.
- **Provost of this era:** Girolamo Armiraglio (son of Battista, of Olgiate Olona).
- **Places:** Gerenzano, Cislago, Uboldo, Saronno, Mozzate, Olgiate (Olona), Como, Legnano, Carbonate.
- **Chapels/altars:** SS. Pietro e Paolo (high altar), S. Zeno, S. Stefano (Crivelli juspatronato),
  S. Croce, S. Raffaele, oratory of S. Giacomo (Fagnani), S. Vittore (ruined), S. Antonio.

## Doc type & formulae
Visitation acts: *"… die … visitavit collegiatam ecclesiam et curatam SS. Petri et Pauli loci Gerenzani,
et primo …"* then item-by-item: Sanctissimum Sacramentum (pyx/tabernacle), fonte battesimale, altari,
reliquie, paramenti, redditi, oneri, decreta. Heavy Latin abbreviation.

## Pitfalls
- Long-s (ſ) reads as f. **"Gerenzani"** is often contracted — it is NOT "Genzani".
- Near-blank pages are struck with a big diagonal line — transcribe only the real text; the strike = a
  cancelled blank, not content. Bleed-through is not text.
- Figures/ages faint or cut at the margin → `[?]`/leave blank, never invent.
