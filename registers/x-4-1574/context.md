## What this register is
- **Visitation / benefice volume of the prepositural church of Gerenzano** (church of
  **SS. Pietro e Paolo**; also "S. Pietro"), **Pieve d'Appiano**, diocese of Milan.
- Label dates it 1574; bound-in documents span **1566–1596**. Mixed **early-modern Italian
  (Lombard clerical)** and **documentary Latin**.
- ~2 MP phone photos — dense cursive, faded ink, ages/figures often in a right margin partly
  cut by the crop. Several near-blank divider/section pages (hallucination risk).

## Document types seen
Land/parcel registers & the prepositura's estate cadastre (parcel name + size in *pertiche* +
bordering owners on each side: *a mane*=E, *a meridie*=S, *ab occasu solis*=W); perpetual-legacy /
*decima* (tithe) obligations; chapel grain-accounts (S. Stefano); church **book inventory** and
**vestment/plate inventories**; building-expense accounts; canon/canonicate income lists; the
**1566 Latin "Status ecclesiae"**; **Cardinal Borromeo sequestration mandates**; a **1574 witness
deposition** (the historic *seven canons / seven altars*); the provost's reports to the Bishop
(confession/communion lists, parish observances, demographics).

## Stock FORMULAE (expect these — helps segment & read)
- Cadastre: *"(E) più possie(de) una terra detta/giacente … pertiche N; coherencie d'una parte …
  a mane … a meridie … ab occasu solis …"*
- Obligation: *"[name] (si tenghi / per tenersi) in pagar ogni anno perpetuamente a questa chiesa
  stara/libre N misura legale di miglio/segale sopra una pezza di terra appellata … in virtù del
  legato … et il parroco facci ogni possibile diligenza di trovar et esibire … il legato."*
- Sequestration (Latin): *"De mandato … D. Caroli Cardinalis Borrhomaei … praecipitur infrascriptis
  debitoribus … in sequestrum teneant … sub poena scutorum 200 auri … et excommunicationis."*

## PITFALLS (relearned, costly — don't repeat)
- **Long-s (ſ) reads as f**: "faruffini"↔"farissini", "Vgerate" etc. Don't over-trust a lone f.
- Ages/figures live in a **right margin often faint or cut by the crop** → leave blank or [?], do NOT
  invent. Some pages the scribe **left ages blank entirely** (that's faithful, not a gap).
- **Near-blank / divider pages** (a parish label + a folio number; bleed-through from the facing leaf)
  → transcribe ONLY the few real words; bleed-through is NOT text. (Cf. the p057 X18 hallucination.)
- Tens-digit ambiguity (16 vs 26) and 5↔8 are common at 2 MP — mark [?] rather than guess.
- "p" = per/par; "q" = que/quon; "9br"=Novembre, "xbr"=Dicembre, "7br"=Settembre; "m.o"=messer/maestro;
  "p.p.to/ppto"=preposito; "fr.llo/f.llo"=fratello; "M./m.a"=moggia; "st"=staro/staia; "R"=rubbo.
