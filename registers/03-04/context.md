# Glossary — Archivio Parrocchiale di Gerenzano (fondi VI–XII)

Parish archive of **Gerenzano** (church of **SS. Pietro e Paolo**, prepositural/collegiate),
**Pieve d'Appiano**, diocese of Milan, on the Como/Varese border. Material spans **c. 1430–1850**:
late-medieval notarial Latin → early-modern Lombard-clerical Italian → 19th-century administrative
Italian. Use to resolve hard proper names/places/formulae **only when the pixels support it — the
image always wins.** `[?]` = one uncertain word, `[…]` = an illegible run; never invent. Several of
these photos cut a margin slightly out of frame → mark edge words `[?]`/`[…]`, don't guess.

## Place
Gerenzano; Pieve d'Appiano. Nearby: Cislago, Turate, Uboldo, Saronno (Serono), Mozzate, Carbonate,
Rescaldina, Bregnano, Olgiate Olona, Legnano, Fagnano. Church chapels/altars: maggiore (SS. Pietro
e Paolo), S. Caterina, S. Zeno(ne), **S. Stefano** (iuspatronato Crivelli), the campestre **S. Giacomo**
(Fagnani oratory), S. Vittore, S. Ambrogio (ruined), Cappella del Cimitero (19th c.).

## Families (recurring; keep the scribe's spelling)
**Crivelli / de Cribellis**; **Gibelli / de Gibellis** (DISTINCT family — do NOT merge with Crivelli);
**Fagnani** (later the **Marchese Fagnani**; aliases **Faruffini, Ramponzani, di Louchi**); **Caimo /
Caymo**; **Carcano**; **Visconti** (= Vicecomes); **Castiglioni**; **Corno** (= de Comitibus / de'
Comiti); **Moneta**; **de Ferrari**; **Serafini**; **Bossi**; **Carnelli**; **Galimberti**; **Bonomi**;
**Rimoldi**; **Torri**; Caccia, Villa, Colliati, Besuti.

## People across eras
- **Girolamo (Hieronimo) Armiraglio / Amiraglio**, provost from 1566 (D.U.D., of Olgiate Olona; ordained
  by Bp **Melchiorre Crivelli**) — often misread "Crivellus"/"Formiaglio".
- **Card. Carlo Borromeo** (visited 1574); **Vincenzo Antonino** (1579 visitor); **Bernardino Taurino**
  J.U.D. (1583 visitor). **Pompeo Crivelli** = non-resident S. Stefano chapel titular (deprived).
- **Giuseppe Trizio (Joseph Tritius)** = provost 1662. Pope **Alexander VII** (1662 procuration).
- 19th c.: Prevosto **Luigi Garavaglia**; the **Marchese Fagnani**; agrimensore **Pietro Tagliabue**;
  **Pietro Gaggia** (Milan procuratore); **Carlo Rimoldi**, **Torri** (1823 *compromesso*); fabbriceri.
- Relics docs (1642, Pope **Urban VIII**): **Valeriano de Chinchellis**, Bp of Terentino; canonico
  **Francesco Dardano** (Milan); relics incl. a **dente di S. Ambrogio** and a button of Christ's robe.

## Document types & formulae in this archive
*legati* (bequests / annuali di messe), *decime* (tithes), *contenzioso* (litigation), *benefici*,
*inventari* (church furnishings/suppellettile), relic authentications, *culto*, *historia*. Land:
**pertiche/perticarum**; grain: **moggia, staia, mistura** (segale+miglio). Boundaries: *coheret a
mane / a meridie / a sera / a monte* = E / S / W / N. *q[uon]d[am]* = the late (fu). Money: lire/soldi/
denari imperiali (L./s./d.), scudi, £ italiane (19th c.).
