# Context — Testamento di Ambrogio Fagnani (16th-c. notarial Latin), pp. p695–p708

**This register**: the will (*testamentum*) of **Ambrogio Fagnani (Ambrosius de Fagnano /
Faruffinus)**, 16th century, in **notarial Latin** — a professional notary's cursive with heavy
conventional abbreviation (expand inside [brackets]: p[er], q[uon]d[am], d[omi]n[u]s, eccl[es]ia,
t[esta]m[en]tum, etc.). Expect the classic testament apparatus: invocation, date by indiction,
sanity formula (*sanus mente, sensu, loquela…*), revocation of prior wills, burial election,
pious bequests (*legata pro anima*), heir institution, fideicommissum/substitutions, witnesses
list (*testes noti et idonei*), notarial subscription and sign.

## External prosopography (from published Gerenzano/San Giacomo histories — 2026-07-23 web pass)
Use to CONFIRM/DENY hard reads at the pixels; NEVER write a web-sourced word as if read — mark it.
- **DATE = 1512** (Millesimo quingentesimo DUODECIMO), indictione XV, die lune 18 octobris. The
  legacy Gemini read "1517/decimo septimo" was WRONG (corrected on p695): indiction XV falls in 1512,
  not 1517, AND published sources date Ambrogio's will to 1512. So expect **"duodecimo"** wherever the
  year recurs — watch for it being misread as "decimo septimo".
- **Testator**: Ambrogio Fagnani (Ambrosius de Fagnano dictus Faruffinus), noble Milanese family with
  possessions in Gerenzano; *filius q[uondam] Iohannis* (per the will). He ENLARGED the church of
  **S. Giacomo** and raised its bell tower. The church's construction is attributed to **Giacomo
  Fagnani**, begun early 1500s (art-hist. study: Biscottini & Crippa, 1989).
- **Documented bequests / content anchors** (look for these in the dispositive section):
  · leaves ALL his Gerenzano assets, incl. the little church of S. Giacomo, to his heirs;
  · commission to COMPLETE an **altarpiece** (ancona): *Madonna in trono col Bambino* + Sts **Peter,
    Paul, James (Iacobus), John the Baptist** + a little music-making angel (attrib. Giovanni Agostino
    da Lodi);
  · commission to COMPLETE the **presbytery vault frescoes**: the Church Fathers (Dottori) alternating
    with the four Evangelists, God the Father (Deus Pater) at the centre.
- **NOT found online** (⇒ pixel-only, cannot be web-confirmed): the NOTARY's name; the names of
  Ambrogio's wife, children and heirs; the witnesses; the sums. The p695 legacy list (son presb.
  Iohannes + daughters Caterina/Margarita/Isabella/Lucrezia/Beatrice/Francesca/Lucia) is Gemini-read,
  NOT externally corroborated — keep every name [?].

## Anchors (verify at the pixels)
- **Fagnani** family, lords in Gerenzano — Latin forms *de Fagnano*, aliases **Faruffini,
  Ramponzani, di Louchi**. Their oratory = the campestre **S. Giacomo** at Gerenzano. Later line
  = the Marchesi Fagnani (a Fra' Ambrosio Fagnani, Knight of Malta, appears 1616/1632 in fondo XI).
- Recurring families in the related Legati papers: Sevesi, Cattaneo, Visconti, Crivelli, Ajroldi,
  Bossi, Berra, Vanelli, Morandi, Rimoldi, Pagnano, Galli (cited dates 1543–1896).
- Place: Gerenzano, Pieve d'Appiano, ducato/stato di Milano; nearby Cislago, Turate, Uboldo,
  Saronno, Mozzate, Legnano, Busto Arsizio. Church: SS. Pietro e Paolo (prepositural).
- Money/land: lire/soldi/denari imperiali, scudi; pertiche; moggia/staia (grain rents).
- Boundaries: *coheret a mane / a meridie / a sera / a monte* = E / S / W / N.

## Notarial-Latin cautions
- Names are often in genitive/ablative — keep the case as written; expansions in [brackets].
- *q[uon]d[am]* = "the late"; *ser/dominus/magister* are titles, not names.
- Roman-numeral sums and dates (e.g. *M.D.LXXIII*) — transcribe exactly; never modernize digits.
- The notary's marginal paragraph numbers / *signum tabellionis* → notes, not transcription lines.
